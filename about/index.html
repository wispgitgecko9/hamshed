What is a ham shed?

A ham shed is a simple thing: it's the place where your amateur radio journey starts.
Maybe it's a real shed in your backyard, like it is for me; maybe it's a cabinet or drawer with your equipment; maybe it's just the radio in your hand. We all have to start somewhere.

What is this Ham Shed?

This is my Ham Shed: a place to share my stories, useful information, and encourage the next generation of ham radio operators.
