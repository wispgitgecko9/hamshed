What is a ham shed?

A ham shed is a simple thing: the place where your amateur radio journey starts. Maybe it's a real shed in your backyard, like it is for me; maybe it's a cabinet or drawer with your equipment; maybe it's in your imagination! We all have to start somewhere.

What is this Ham Shed?

This Ham Shed is a place for a new generation to discover the world of amateur radio.
